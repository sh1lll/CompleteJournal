Settings

Live connection (optional)

Fills appear here within about a second of happening. Read-only: Complete Journal can never place, change or cancel orders. Importing files keeps working for every platform. Credentials are kept in your system keychain (or a private file only you can read), never in the journal.

Phone quick-capture

Jot notes and snap photos from your phone during the session; they land here as intraday entries. Works on your home Wi-Fi. A paired phone can only add notes — it can't read your journal. Notes typed without signal are kept on the phone and sent later.

Updates

AI (optional)

Complete Journal works fully without AI. "Seen this before?" and the daily recap run on this computer. If you add an Anthropic API key, you also get written AI summaries and "Ask your journal". AI text is always kept separate from your own writing and cites its sources.

Import trades

Drop in any export from any platform — Tradovate, TopstepX/ProjectX, NinjaTrader, Rithmic R|Trader, TradingView, or anything else with fills or trades in it. The format, delimiter, number style and date order are detected automatically. Re-importing the same file is always safe.
